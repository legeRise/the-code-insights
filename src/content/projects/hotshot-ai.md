Hotshot AI is an AI customer assistant built around HotShotSecret.com. I built it to help customers understand the site's products and get useful answers without having to navigate everything themselves.

It can answer product questions, guide customers toward suitable products, make product recommendations, explain site information, help with FAQs, and assist with diagnosing truck-related problems.

## What it does

- Answers product and site questions
- Recommends products based on customer needs
- Guides customers through product information
- Handles FAQ-style customer support
- Helps diagnose truck-related problems

## Stack

Python, Flask, OpenAI, REST APIs, and conversational AI workflows.
