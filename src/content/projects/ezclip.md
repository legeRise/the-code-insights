EzClip is an AI-powered video generation backend built to turn a script into a finished narrated video. It coordinates AI-generated visuals, narration, captions, cinematic effects, concurrent generation, and live progress updates.

## Stack

Django, Django REST Framework, Celery, Redis, OpenAI, Runware, MoviePy, SSE, and Cloudflare R2.
